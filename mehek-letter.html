Meri pyaari Mehek,

Kai baar socha ki tujhe yeh sab bolunga — yeh sab jo mere andar itne time se bhar ke baitha hai — par lafz hamesha chote lagte rahe. Aaj likh raha hoon, kyunki kuch feelings ko sirf khamoshi mein nahi rehne dena chahiye.

Tujhe shayad pata bhi nahi hoga, but the way you exist in my life is something I can never fully explain. Tu woh insaan hai jo bina kuch pooche samajh jaati hai. Bura waqt ho ya achha — teri presence mein ek ajeeb sa sukoon hai, jo na words se aata hai, na kisi effort se. Bas hota hai. Aur yeh jo "bas hota hai" waali feeling hai — yeh bohot rare hai, Mehek. Bohot rare.

Zindagi ne kabhi kabhi aisa mahaul banaya jahan sab kuch bhari bhari lagti thi — andar se ek thakaan thi jo bahar se nahi dikhti. Unhi dino mein, tujhse ek chhoti si baat karna, ya sirf tujhe paas hona — woh itna kaafi hota tha. Tu kabhi judge nahi karti, kabhi lecture nahi deti. Tu sunti hai. Sachchi waali sunna — woh jo bahut kam logon ko aata hai. Aur yeh, is duniya mein, ek bohot badi cheez hai.

Khushi ke lamhe bhi tere saath zyada gehra rang lete hain. Chhoti chhoti baatein — wo hassi jo achanak aati hai, wo conversations jo kisi topic pe shuru hoti hai aur kisi aur jagah khatam hoti hai — yeh moments mere paas ek khaas jagah rakhte hain. Tere saath waqt guzaarna kabhi "time pass" nahi laga. Hamesha meaningful laga.

Aur yeh jo humare beech ek silent understanding hai — jab ek nazar se sab keh diya jaata hai, jab baat adhoori chhod di jaaye aur phir bhi sab samajh aaye — yeh woh cheez hai jo sirf kuch hi rishton mein hoti hai. Main isse kabhi grant nahi leta, aur kabhi lena bhi nahi chahta. Yeh bond mujhe aziz hai — us tarah, jaise koi apni sabse porani, sabse pyaari cheez ko sambhal ke rakhta hai, carefully, lovingly.

Sacchi dosti ka koi certificate nahi hota, koi grand gesture zaruri nahi hoti. Woh hoti hai un chhote chhote palo mein jab koi tere liye wahan hota hai — bina reason ke, bina kisi expectation ke. Tu woh hai mere liye. Aur yeh acknowledgement hi meri taraf se sabse sachchi thank you hai jo main de sakta hoon.

Teri friendship ne mujhe yeh sikhaaya hai ki duniya mein kuch log hote hain jo tumhare andar ki aachi cheezein bahar laate hain — without even trying. Tu unhi logon mein se hai. Tere hone se meri zindagi genuinely better hai. Yeh main puri tareh, puri imaandaari se bol raha hoon.

Toh yeh letter sirf ek "thank you" nahi hai — yeh ek acknowledgement hai ki tu meri zindagi ka ek aise hissa hai jis par mujhe genuinely, deeply, garv hai. Aur chahe waqt kitna bhi badal jaaye, yeh nahi badlega.
